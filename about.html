About Stackly

A family jewellery house built on honest gold, certified stones and meticulous craft.

Our Story

Born in a small workshop, driven by a big promise

Stackly began in 2009 with one belief: that fine jewellery should come with honesty. Every gram weighed in front of you. Every diamond graded and documented.

Today our artisans still sit at the same benches, now supported by modern 3D design and laser tools, but every piece is still finished by hand.

Our Journey

Fifteen years, one standard

  • 2009

    A small workshop

    Stackly opens as a two-artisan gold workshop in MVP Colony, Visakhapatnam.

  • 2013

    Diamond studio

    We add a certified-diamond studio and begin offering IGI-graded solitaires.

  • 2018

    Bridal atelier

    Our bridal atelier launches with fully customised wedding sets.

  • 2022

    Online presence

    Stackly goes digital with insured delivery across India.

  • 2026

    Next chapter

    A new flagship experience and expanded custom design studio.
